## 2. Culinary

"At Kempinski, gastronomy is at the heart of everything we do. Our chefs combine decades of international expertise with extensive knowledge of local cuisines and cultures to offer a unique exploration of flavours."

- Chefs hold Michelin-star recognition at select properties

## 4. Sport & Adventure

"Enhance your stay and give yourself a boost. Enjoy a spectacular range of sport and adventure experiences at Kempinski."

- **Winter sports:** Alpine skiing and winter activities at European mountain properties (St. Moritz, Berchtesgaden, Salzburg)
- **Water sports:** Catamaran sailing, diving, snorkelling, and beach activities at coastal/island resorts (Bali, Seychelles, Cancún)
- **Golf:** Multiple Kempinski properties with on-site or nearby championship golf courses
- URL: https://www.kempinski.com/en/inspirations/sport-and-adventure/golf
