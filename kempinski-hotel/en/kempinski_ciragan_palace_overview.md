# Çırağan Palace Kempinski Istanbul — Property Overview

---

## 1. The Property

**"The Grande Dame of the Bosphorus"**

Çırağan Palace Kempinski Istanbul is the **only authentic Ottoman Imperial Palace and luxury 5-star hotel on the Bosphorus**. It occupies one of the most prestigious and historic addresses in Istanbul — the former Imperial Palace of the Ottoman Sultans.

- **Room count:** 317 rooms (described as a "resort atmosphere in the city")
- **Category:** 5-star luxury, historic palace hotel
- **Setting:** On the European shore of the Bosphorus strait, Beşiktaş district
- **Google Maps:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=41.04461,29.01662

---

## 2. Key Highlights

- Only Ottoman imperial palace converted into a hotel, directly on the Bosphorus
- Resort-style amenities within a city hotel (outdoor heated infinity pool, full spa)
- Awarded **"Türkiye's Best MICE Hotel"** by the World MICE Awards
- Historic architecture blended with modern luxury
- Access to Bosphorus waterfront with pier
- Direct views of the strait from many rooms, restaurants, and event spaces
- Within the Beşiktaş neighbourhood — close to Yıldız Park, Ortaköy Mosque, and city centre

## 5. Location Context (Istanbul)

Istanbul straddles two continents (Europe and Asia) and is Türkiye's largest city. The Bosphorus strait divides the city. Çırağan Palace sits on the European shore in Beşiktaş, one of Istanbul's most vibrant and central neighbourhoods.

**Nearby attractions:**
- Yıldız Park — adjacent (some rooms overlook it)
- Ortaköy Mosque — walking distance
- Dolmabahçe Palace — ~1.5 km
- Taksim Square — ~5 km
- Grand Bazaar — ~8 km
- Topkapı Palace — ~10 km
- Hagia Sophia — ~10 km
- Atatürk Airport / Istanbul Airport — accessible by road
