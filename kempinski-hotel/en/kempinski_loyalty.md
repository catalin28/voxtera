# KEMPINSKI DISCOVERY Loyalty Programme

---

## Programme Name

**KEMPINSKI DISCOVERY** (part of Global Hotel Alliance GHA DISCOVERY)

---

## How to Join

Free to join. Sign up at: https://www.kempinski.com/en/loyalty/join-now
Or download the Kempinski app.

---

## Tiers

| Tier | Qualification |
|---|---|
| **Silver** | Upon joining — instant membership |
| **Gold** | Stay 2 times OR spend USD 1,000 |
| **Platinum** | Stay 10 nights OR spend USD 5,000 OR stay with 2 brands |
| **Titanium** | Stay 30 nights OR spend USD 15,000 OR stay with 3 brands |

**Instant benefits begin the moment you join** (Silver tier).

---

## DISCOVERY Dollars (D$)

- Earn D$ as a percentage back on eligible spend at participating hotels
- Redeem D$ as currency at any hotel in the GHA DISCOVERY global collection
- D$ can be earned and spent across 950+ hotels, 50+ brands worldwide
- **Exception:** Earning/redeeming D$ is not possible at Kempinski properties in Russia; redeeming is not possible in St. Moritz, Switzerland

---

## Global Reach

KEMPINSKI DISCOVERY recognition extends beyond Kempinski hotels to **950+ hotels from 50+ brands worldwide** through Global Hotel Alliance (GHA). Members receive recognition and can earn/spend D$ at any property in the collection.

Explore all brands: https://www.ghadiscovery.com/Our-Brands
