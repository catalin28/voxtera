# Kempinski Hotels — History & Timeline

---

## Founding

Kempinski was founded in **Berlin in 1897** by Berthold Kempinski, a wine merchant and restaurateur. His first wine restaurant at Leipziger Strasse 25 became a social hub featuring multiple elegant dining halls, including the opulent Emperor's Hall inaugurated by Kaiser Wilhelm II.

The brand is **Europe's oldest luxury hotel group**, with 125+ years of continuous operation.

---

## Key Milestones

### 1907 — The Berlin Renaissance
The first Kempinski wine restaurant at Kurfuerstendamm becomes a dining landmark in Berlin. Known for affordable luxuries, daily set menus, and seasonal delicacies (lobster, crayfish, fine wines by the glass).

### 1928 — Beautiful Performances Born
The spectacular **Haus Vaterland** at Potsdamer Platz opens — an entertainment destination with themed dining rooms representing cuisines from around the world. Kempinski establishes its reputation for unique, theatrical experiences.

### 1952 / 1961 — Enters the Hotel Business
Dr Friedrich Unger (Berthold Kempinski's grandson) transforms the Kurfuerstendamm restaurant into **Hotel Bristol Kempinski**, the first new luxury hotel in post-war West Berlin. It becomes the city's social centre — US President John F. Kennedy famously declared "Ich bin ein Berliner" during his 1963 Berlin visit while staying at the Bristol.

### 1977 — Golden Age of Luxury
European expansion accelerates with **Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten Kempinski Munich** and Hotel Gravenbruch near Frankfurt. Lavish décor and first-class amenities define a new Golden Age of luxury travel.

### 1992 — Asia Expansion: China
A new hotel in Beijing's diplomatic district opens, offering European luxury as a gateway for business travellers. Kempinski grows across China and the wider Asia-Pacific region.

### 2005 — Arabian Peninsula
Kempinski establishes itself in the Middle East with properties in Ajman, Abu Dhabi, and Dubai (Mall of the Emirates, with iconic Swiss chalet-inspired penthouses overlooking Ski Dubai). Further expansion across Saudi Arabia, Oman, Jordan, and Egypt follows.

### 2013 — Africa
The first Kempinski luxury safari camp opens in **Kenya's Masai Mara**. Additional hotels open in Nairobi, Accra, and Djibouti. The Kempinski Seychelles Resort Baie Lazare brings Indian Ocean luxury to the portfolio.

### 2017 — The Americas
**Gran Hotel Manzana Kempinski La Habana** becomes the first true luxury hotel in Havana, Cuba. North America follows with Kempinski Hotel Cancún, México.

### Present
Kempinski operates 75+ properties across 35+ countries, partnered with Global Hotel Alliance (GHA DISCOVERY) for worldwide loyalty recognition.

## Brand Philosophy

> "Ever since 1897, we have travelled in style, creating defining moments and beautiful performances for our guests. From the charms of the Belle Epoque to the extravagances of the opulent 70s, Kempinski has evolved through the ages, keeping pace with trends in fashion and design that defined each era."

The brand's core belief: **prestigious heritage shapes a bright future**.
