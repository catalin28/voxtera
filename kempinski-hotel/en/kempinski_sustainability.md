# Kempinski Hotels — Sustainability & ESG

---

## Approach

"We acknowledge that the travel industry must accelerate efforts to mitigate its contribution to climate change."

Kempinski has been working with professional consultancies and third-party providers on sustainable initiatives for many years. In **2021**, the **Sustainability Committee** was formally established to implement a comprehensive sustainability policy across all Kempinski Group subsidiaries.

---

## Three Pillars

| Pillar | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Environmental** | Climate change mitigation, energy, water, waste, biodiversity |
| **Social** | Community engagement, employee wellbeing, cultural preservation |
| **Governance** | Policies, reporting, compliance, transparency |
