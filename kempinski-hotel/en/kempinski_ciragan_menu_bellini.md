# Bellini — Menu

The palace's Italian restaurant, set in the historic palace building. Some dishes offer a pork option.

## Starters

- **"Bruschetta"** (vegan option) — Bread, tomato, olive oil, basil, garlic, black pepper
- **"Burrata" Salad** — Seasonal greens, red fruits, olives
- **Sweet & Sour Sicilian Eggplant** — Caponata cheese, olives, fried risotto
- **Octopus "Carpaccio"** — Olives, capers, tomato, arugula
- **Beef "Carpaccio"** — Parmesan, burrata, arugula, basil sauce
- **Tuna Trio** — Carpaccio, battuta, grilled olives, lemon sauce
- **Sicilian-Style Cold Soup** — Tomato, basil, cucumber, onion
- **"Caprese" Salad** — Heirloom tomatoes, olives, mozzarella, basil sauce
- **Charcuterie Platter** (pork option) — Italian charcuterie, assorted cheeses
- **Lobster Grilled Vegetable Terrine** — Zucchini, eggplant, peppers, red onion, vegetable sauce
- **Land & Sea Discovery** — Fried prawns, eggplant parmesan, saffron arancini, smoked beef, burrata, tomato
