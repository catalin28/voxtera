## 1. What is Kempinski?

Kempinski Hotels is Europe's oldest luxury hotel group, founded in Berlin in 1897. It operates a portfolio of five-star hotels, resorts, and residences in more than 35 countries across Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia, and the Americas. Each Kempinski property balances European luxury with authentic local culture.

**Tagline / brand promise:** "Destinations to discover, journeys to remember."

**Booking:** Guests are encouraged to book direct at kempinski.com for the best rates (Price Match Promise / Best Rate Guarantee).

## 2. Brand Positioning

- **Category:** Ultra-luxury, five-star
- **Differentiator:** European heritage (since 1897) combined with bespoke local flavour at every property
- **Key pillars:** Personalised service, iconic locations, culinary excellence, wellness, sensational suites
- **Signature service:** "Lady in Red" — a dedicated guest relations ambassador present at every Kempinski hotel worldwide, recognisable by her red uniform. She handles everything a guest could wish for with warmth, charisma, care, and attention to detail.
- **Scale:** 75+ hotels and resorts; portfolio spans more than 35 countries
