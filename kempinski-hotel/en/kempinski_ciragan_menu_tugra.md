# Tuğra Restaurant — Menu

Ottoman and Turkish fine dining ("Taâmât-ı Sâhil-i Hâssa" by Chef Onur Dönmez). Michelin Guide recommended; Gault&Millau "Outstanding Table" three toques. Dinner Mon–Sun 19:00–23:00. Reservation required. Children under 12 are not admitted.

## Starters

- **"Çeşm-i Nigâr" Soup** — "Mardin" red lentils, lentil crackers, buttered mint
- **Lamb's Head Ballotine** — Sweetbread cream, pickled onion, herb salad
- **Turkish Cold "Meze" Platter** — Sautéed green beans (string beans); stuffed vine leaves (rice stuffing, cherry yoghurt); "Köpoğlu" (eggplant, slow-cooked tomatoes, crispy onions); yellow lentil fava (octopus salad); "Kısır" (cracked wheat, red turnip, pomegranate molasses); hummus (dried fruits)
- **"Lakerda"** — Red onion purée, mint and yoghurt sorbet
- **Anatolian Cheese Platter** — "Karaman Obruk, İzmir Tulum, Kars Gruyère, Çanakkale Ezine, Denizli Kale" pepper cheese; slow-cooked dried fruits, homemade sourdough bread, walnut, quince purée
- **"Bostana" Salad** — Arugula, red lettuce, pickled beetroot, farm cheese, dried tomatoes
- **"Yazok" Meat Börek** — Pastrami, smoked eggplant, yoghurt with zahter
- **Beef Liver** — "Baklava" dough cheese börek and "Kapıdağ" red onion with sumac
- **"Mantı"** — Minced meat, tomato sauce, "Bolu keş" cheese, buffalo cream yoghurt
- **"Zemher-i" Cabbage** — "Keme" mushroom, celery purée, onion sauce with molasses

## Main Courses

- **"Hünkâr Beğendi" 2.0** (signature) — Lamb shoulder, crispy eggplant
- **Chicken "Topkapı"** (signature) — Rice stuffing, mashed potatoes, creamy spinach, bone jus
- **Lamb Shank** — "Keşkek", "Geyve" quince, dried plum, "isot" oil
- **Heifer Tenderloin** — "Müceddere" rice, carrot purée, asparagus, marrow sauce
- **"Testi Kebap"** — Lamb leg, root vegetables, "Dane-i sade" rice
- **Black Sea Turbot** — Sour cherry purée, potato pavé, fennel salad, ginger whey sauce
- **Vegetable "Türlü"** (vegan) — Mushroom patties, black chickpeas, mung bean
