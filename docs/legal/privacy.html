Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

Voxtera operates a multilingual voice and messaging concierge for hospitality. This policy explains what personal data we process when you interact with Voxtera — by voice, on the web, or through messaging channels such as WhatsApp — and the choices you have.

This policy applies to Voxtera, operated by 9285‑3381 Québec inc. ("Voxtera", "we", "us"), a company registered in Québec, Canada. For properties that deploy Voxtera, the hotel or operator is the data controller for their guests' data and Voxtera acts as their data processor under a Data Processing Agreement.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Conversation content — the messages you send and the voice audio you speak when you contact a Voxtera‑powered service, and our replies.
  • Contact identifiers — for messaging channels, your phone number or WhatsApp ID; for web or email, the address you use to reach us.
  • Request details — information you share to be helped (e.g. a room number, a booking reference, a preference, a complaint).

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — timestamps, language detected, message and call metadata, and diagnostic logs needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Derived data — transcripts and structured summaries generated from a conversation so a request can be actioned by the property's team.

2. How we use information

  • To understand your request and respond in your language, in real time.
  • To create tickets and summaries that the property's staff can action (e.g. via Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, or the property's management system).
  • To route safety, medical, or emergency matters to a human without delay.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data under one or more of: performance of a contract (delivering the concierge service you requested), legitimate interests (operating and securing the service), consent (where required, such as recording where applicable), and legal obligation.

4. Voice recordings

Voice interactions are processed to detect language, understand the request, and generate a reply and summary. Audio recording and retention can be disabled on a per‑property basis. Where a property enables recording, it is responsible for obtaining any consent required in its jurisdiction.

5. Messaging channels (WhatsApp and others)

When you message a Voxtera number on WhatsApp, your message is delivered to us through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. Meta's handling of your data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. We use your number and message solely to respond to your request and to log it for the property's team. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

6. How we share information

  • The property you contacted — your request, transcript, and summary are shared with the hotel or operator so they can serve you.
  • Service providers (subprocessors) — we use trusted providers for speech recognition, language models, text‑to‑speech, hosting, and messaging delivery, under contractual data‑protection obligations. A current list is available on request.
  • Legal and safety — where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the public.

We do not sell personal data.

7. International transfers

Voxtera is deployed in the region closest to the property where practical. Where personal data is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Data retention

We keep conversation data only as long as needed to deliver the service, fulfil the request, meet the property's configured retention period, and comply with legal obligations — after which it is deleted or anonymised. Properties may configure shorter retention or disable storage of audio.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES‑256). Access is restricted on a need‑to‑know basis. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we maintain safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or object to/restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because the property is usually the controller of guest data, we will forward your request to the relevant property and assist them in responding. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

11. Children

Voxtera is intended for use by adults in a hospitality context and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through the service or to the operating property.
