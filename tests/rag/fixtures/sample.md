# Grand Voxtera Resort & Spa

Welcome to the Grand Voxtera Resort & Spa, located on the stunning Amalfi Coast.

## Check-in & Check-out

- **Check-in:** 3:00 PM
- **Check-out:** 11:00 AM
- Early check-in available upon request (subject to availability).
- Late check-out until 2:00 PM for a fee of €30.

## Breakfast

Breakfast is served daily in the Terrazza Restaurant:

- **Weekdays:** 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM
- **Weekends:** 7:30 AM – 11:00 AM

The buffet includes fresh pastries, local cheeses, seasonal fruit, eggs to order,
and a selection of Italian cured meats. Gluten-free and vegan options available.

## Swimming Pool

The outdoor infinity pool is open from **8:00 AM to 10:00 PM** daily.
Towels are provided at the pool deck. Children under 12 must be accompanied
by an adult. The pool bar serves drinks and light snacks from 10 AM to 8 PM.

## Spa & Wellness

The Serenità Spa offers:

- Hot stone massage (60 min) — €90
- Couples aromatherapy (90 min) — €160
- Turkish hammam experience — €45
- Facial treatments starting at €55

Appointments can be booked at reception or by dialling extension 200.

## Wi-Fi

Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel.

- **Network:** GrandVoxtera-Guest
- **Password:** provided at check-in

## Shuttle Service

A complimentary shuttle runs to Positano and Ravello:

| Destination | Departure | Return  |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Positano    | 9:00 AM   | 5:00 PM |
| Ravello     | 10:00 AM  | 4:00 PM |

Advance booking required at the front desk.
