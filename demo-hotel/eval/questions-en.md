# Evaluation Questions — English

Each entry has a guest question, the expected source document, and the expected answer with key facts the bot should include.

## Menu & Dining

### Q1: What time is breakfast served?
- **Source:** menu, policies
- **Expected answer:** Breakfast at Le Mirador is served 6:30–10:30 daily, weekends until 11:00. Buffet price is €38 per adult, €19 per child (4–12), free under 4.

### Q2: What are the signature dishes at dinner?
- **Source:** menu
- **Expected answer:** Signature dishes include Bouillabaisse Marseillaise, Coq au vin "Lumière", Magret de canard with cherry jus, Risotto aux truffes noires (Dec–Feb), and Tarte fine aux pommes. A 6-course tasting menu (Menu Découverte) is €95, wine pairing +€55.

### Q3: Is there a place for casual dining?
- **Source:** menu
- **Expected answer:** La Petite Terrasse is the garden bistro, open 12:00–23:00. Serves salads, sandwiches, croque monsieur, steak frites, and wood-fired pizzas from 18:00. Children's menu available.

### Q4: Can I get food late at night?
- **Source:** menu, room-service-ordering
- **Expected answer:** Room service is available 24 hours. Between 23:00 and 06:30, a reduced menu (sandwiches, soups, omelettes, pastries) is served. A €5 service charge applies per order.

## Spa & Wellness

### Q5: How much is a couples massage?
- **Source:** spa
- **Expected answer:** The couples massage is 60 minutes in the double suite and costs €290.

### Q6: What are the pool hours?
- **Source:** spa
- **Expected answer:** The indoor heated pool (18m, 29°C) is open 6:30–22:00 daily. Children under 16 are welcome between 8:00 and 11:00 only. The thermal area (sauna, hammam) is adults only.

### Q7: Are there yoga classes at the hotel?
- **Source:** spa
- **Expected answer:** Yes. Yoga is offered Mon, Wed, Fri at 7:30 (45 min). Aqua-fit Tue & Thu at 8:00 (30 min). Pilates Mat Sat at 10:00 (60 min). All classes are complimentary for hotel guests; reserve at the spa desk.

## Spa Booking

### Q8: What is the cancellation policy for spa treatments?
- **Source:** spa-booking-policy
- **Expected answer:** Free cancellation up to 12 hours before appointment. Within 12 hours: 50% fee. No-show: 100% fee. One complimentary reschedule is allowed if requested at least 12 hours in advance.

### Q9: How do I book a spa treatment?
- **Source:** spa-booking-policy
- **Expected answer:** You can book through the hotel assistant (voice or chat), at the Spa Reception desk, or via the Front Desk concierge. You'll need to provide guest name, room number, treatment type, preferred date/time, and number of guests.

## Policies

### Q10: What time is check-in and check-out?
- **Source:** policies
- **Expected answer:** Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 12:00 (noon). Late check-out until 14:00 is complimentary when available; until 18:00 is 50% room rate; after 18:00 is a full additional night.

### Q11: What is the Wi-Fi password?
- **Source:** policies, troubleshooting
- **Expected answer:** Network is Lumiere-Guest. The password is printed on your keycard sleeve at check-in. A premium streaming-quality network (Lumiere-Plus) is available for €10/day or free for Lumière Club members.

### Q12: Can I bring my dog?
- **Source:** policies
- **Expected answer:** Small pets under 10 kg are welcome for €40 per night. Pet bed, bowls, and treats are provided. Pets are not permitted in restaurants, spa, or pool. Service animals are always welcome at no charge.

## Troubleshooting

### Q13: My TV is not working, what should I do?
- **Source:** troubleshooting
- **Expected answer:** Press the red button on the remote to power on. If no picture, set input to HDMI 1 using the Source button. If frozen, hold power for 5 seconds to restart. If unresolved, dial extension 0 (front desk) or extension 7 (engineering).

### Q14: The air conditioning is not cooling my room.
- **Source:** troubleshooting, maintenance-service-levels
- **Expected answer:** Check the thermostat by the bedroom door (tap to wake, use +/− to set 18–26°C). Make sure windows are closed — the system pauses if a window is open for 60+ seconds. If unresolved, call extension 7 for engineering. AC not cooling is Priority 2 (urgent): 20-minute response, 2-hour resolution target.

### Q15: I forgot my safe code.
- **Source:** troubleshooting
- **Expected answer:** Dial extension 0. A staff member will arrive within 10 minutes with the master key. You will need to show ID.

## Room Service

### Q16: How long does room service delivery take?
- **Source:** room-service-ordering
- **Expected answer:** Typical delivery is 20–35 minutes. A standard delivery fee of €8 applies (no fee for suites/premium packages). During peak breakfast (07:30–09:30) it may take longer. If delivery exceeds 45 minutes, it escalates to the Duty Manager.

### Q17: Can I cancel a room service order?
- **Source:** room-service-ordering
- **Expected answer:** You can modify or cancel within 5 minutes of confirmation. After food preparation starts, cancellation may be charged up to 100% of item value.

## Maintenance

### Q18: There is a water leak in my bathroom.
- **Source:** maintenance-service-levels
- **Expected answer:** A water leak is Priority 1 (critical). Target response is within 10 minutes, resolution within 60 minutes or immediate room move. Call extension 0 or extension 7 immediately.

## Welcome & Local

### Q19: Are there any museums nearby?
- **Source:** welcome-guide
- **Expected answer:** Within 20 minutes' walk: Musée d'Orsay (closed Mondays), Petit Palais (free permanent collection), Musée de l'Orangerie (Monet's Water Lilies, small queues before 11:00), and Musée Jacquemart-André.

### Q20: How do I get to the airport?
- **Source:** welcome-guide
- **Expected answer:** CDG airport: 35–55 min by taxi (€55–75 fixed fare) or 45 min via RER B from Auber. Orly: 25–40 min by taxi (€32–45 fixed fare). Uber and Bolt also operate; the doorman can call one.
