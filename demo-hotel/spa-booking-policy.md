# Spa Booking and Reservation Policy

## Spa Opening Hours
Spa Lumiere is open daily from 08:00 to 21:00.

- Last 60-minute treatment starts at 20:00
- Thermal area closes at 21:00
- Fitness studio closes at 22:00

## Booking Channels
Guests can reserve treatments through:

1. The hotel assistant (voice or chat)
2. Spa Reception desk
3. Front Desk concierge

Required booking details: guest name, room number, treatment type, preferred date and time, and number of guests.

## Appointment Duration and Arrival
Treatment durations are 30, 45, 60, and 90 minutes depending on service.

Guests should arrive 15 minutes before treatment time for check-in and consultation. Late arrivals may reduce treatment time to protect the next booking.

## Payment and Deposit Rules
In-house guests can charge treatments to room.

- Treatments under 100 EUR: no advance deposit required
- Treatments 100 EUR and above: 30% deposit may be required
- Private spa suites and couples packages: full pre-authorization may be required

## Cancellation and Rescheduling
- Free cancellation up to 12 hours before appointment
- Cancellation within 12 hours: 50% fee
- No-show: 100% fee

One complimentary reschedule is allowed if requested at least 12 hours in advance.

## Waitlist and Alternatives
If no slot is available, guests can join a waitlist and receive notifications for cancellations. The assistant should offer up to three nearest available alternatives.

## Health and Safety
Guests must disclose pregnancy, recent surgery, injuries, or skin sensitivities before treatment. Spa staff may adapt or decline a treatment for safety reasons and suggest an alternative.

## Group and Private Bookings
For groups of four or more guests, booking should be made at least 24 hours in advance. Private wellness events require direct confirmation from Spa Management.
