Questions, briefly answered

Things people always ask.

How fast is the response, really?

End-to-end — from the moment the guest stops speaking to the first syllable of Voxtera's reply — averages 600 to 850 milliseconds. That's faster than most human pickups on a hotel phone, and well within the threshold where conversation feels natural.

What if the guest interrupts?

Voxtera stops immediately, listens, and continues the new thread — like a real conversation, not a robocall menu.

How does it know about my property?

During onboarding we ingest your menus, room amenities, hours, house rules, local recommendations and FAQs into a private knowledge layer. Updates take seconds — change your spa hours and Voxtera knows on the next call.

What about escalating to a real person?

Anything safety-related, medical, or emotionally distressed is flagged in real time and routed to a staff member via the channel you specify — Slack, SMS, internal phone. Voxtera stays with the guest until the handover completes.

Where is guest data stored?

Infrastructure is deployed in the region closest to your property — North America, Europe, or other regions as needed. We work with trusted subprocessors and select hosting regions per deployment. Full details on data routing and subprocessors are available on request. All conversations are encrypted in transit and at rest; recordings can be disabled per-property.

Can it handle a phone line, not just web?

Yes. We'll work with you to connect Voxtera to your existing phone system — so the same brain, the same voices, and the same languages reach every guest who calls in.

Can it connect to the systems we already use?

Yes. Voxtera can connect to your management system and open customer tickets automatically — or route every guest request straight to your team's Telegram, WhatsApp, or Slack. Your staff stay in the tools they already know.
