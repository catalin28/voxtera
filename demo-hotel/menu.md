# Restaurant & Dining — Grand Hôtel Lumière

The hotel has two restaurants, a lobby bar, and 24-hour room service.

## Le Mirador (Rooftop Restaurant)

Open daily for **breakfast, lunch, and dinner**.

- Breakfast: **6:30–10:30** (weekends until 11:00)
- Lunch: **12:00–14:30**
- Dinner: **19:00–22:30**

Reservations recommended for dinner. Dial **extension 412** from your room or
ask the concierge.

### Breakfast

A continental and à la carte buffet is served. Highlights:

- Freshly baked **croissants au beurre** and **pains au chocolat** from our
  in-house bakery
- House-made **brioche perdue** with vanilla cream and caramelised pear
- Smoked Scottish salmon with crème fraîche and capers
- Made-to-order **omelette au comté** and Eggs Florentine
- A selection of European cheeses, charcuterie, fresh fruit, yoghurts, and
  pastries
- Espresso, cappuccino, fresh juices, and a small selection of teas

Buffet price: **€38 per adult**, **€19 per child (4–12)**, free under 4.

### Lunch & Dinner — Signature Dishes

- **Bouillabaisse Marseillaise** — saffron-scented Provençal fish stew with
  rouille and grilled baguette
- **Coq au vin "Lumière"** — slow-braised free-range chicken in Bourgogne
  red wine
- **Magret de canard** — pan-seared duck breast with cherry jus and
  pommes Sarladaises
- **Risotto aux truffes noires** (in season, December–February)
- **Tarte fine aux pommes** with Calvados ice cream

A 6-course tasting menu (**Menu Découverte**) is available for **€95 per
guest**, wine pairing **+€55**.

## La Petite Terrasse (Garden Bistro)

Casual all-day dining in the garden courtyard. Open **12:00–23:00**.

- Salads, sandwiches, croque monsieur, steak frites
- Wood-fired pizzas in the evenings (from 18:00)
- Children's menu available

## Bar Lumière (Lobby)

Open **16:00–01:00** (Friday and Saturday until 02:00).

Specialities include the **Lumière 75** (a Champagne cocktail with elderflower
and lemon) and a curated list of single-vintage Armagnacs. Light bites and a
cheese plate are served until midnight.

## Room Service

Available **24 hours**. Dial **extension 0** to order. A reduced overnight
menu (sandwiches, soups, omelettes, pastries) is served between 23:00 and
06:30. A €5 service charge is added per order.

## Dietary Requirements

All restaurants accommodate **vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, and
kosher** requests with prior notice. Please inform the host when reserving or
mention it to your server. Allergens are listed on every menu.
