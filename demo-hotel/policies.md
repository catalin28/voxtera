# Hotel Policies — Grand Hôtel Lumière

## Check-in & Check-out

- **Check-in**: from **15:00**
- **Check-out**: by **12:00 (noon)**
- **Early check-in** and **late check-out** can be requested at the front
  desk and are subject to availability. A late check-out until 14:00 is
  complimentary when available; until 18:00 is charged at 50% of the room
  rate; after 18:00 is a full additional night.
- Photo ID and the credit card used for booking are required at check-in.

## Wi-Fi

Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel.

- Network: **Lumiere-Guest**
- Password: printed on your keycard sleeve at check-in
- A premium **streaming-quality** network (**Lumiere-Plus**) is available on
  request for €10/day per device or complimentary for **Lumière Club**
  members.

## Children

Children of all ages are welcome.

- Cribs and rollaway beds are complimentary; please request 24 hours in
  advance.
- Children **under 12** stay free in their parents' room when using existing
  bedding.
- A children's menu is available at La Petite Terrasse and via room service.
- The pool is open to children **8:00 – 11:00**; the spa thermal area is
  adults only.
- Babysitting can be arranged through the concierge with **24 hours** notice
  (€35 per hour, 4-hour minimum).

## Pets

We welcome small pets (under 10 kg) for an additional **€40 per night**.
Please inform us in advance.

- Pet bed, bowls, and welcome treats are provided.
- Pets are **not permitted** in the restaurants, spa, or pool area.
- Service animals are always welcome at no charge and in all areas.

## Cancellation Policy

- **Flexible rate**: cancel free of charge until **18:00 the day before
  arrival**. After this, one night will be charged.
- **Advance Saver rate**: non-refundable. The full stay is charged at the
  time of booking.
- **No-show**: full first-night charge.
- Cancellations for stays of 5+ nights or during major events (Fashion Week,
  Roland-Garros) follow the terms specified at booking.

## Smoking

The hotel is **entirely non-smoking**, including all rooms, balconies, and
indoor public areas. A €250 cleaning fee applies if smoking is detected in a
room. A designated smoking area is located in the rear courtyard.

## Payment & Identification

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay, and contactless
payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay). Cash is accepted for amounts up to €1,000.

A pre-authorisation of **€200 per night** is placed on your credit card at
check-in to cover incidentals.

## Lost Property

Items left behind are kept for **90 days**. Contact
**lostandfound@grandhotellumiere.example** with your room number and
approximate dates of stay.
