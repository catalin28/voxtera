# Room Service Ordering Guide

## Service Hours
Room service is available daily from 06:00 to 23:00.

- Breakfast menu: 06:00-11:00
- All-day menu: 11:00-23:00
- Late beverage service: 23:00-00:00 (hot and cold drinks only)

Orders placed after 23:00 are prepared for the next available service window unless the request is beverages only.

## How to Place an Order
Guests can place a room service order by:

1. Calling the Room Service Desk from the in-room phone.
2. Asking the hotel assistant in chat or voice.
3. Scanning the in-room QR menu and confirming through chat.

Please provide room number, item names, quantities, and any dietary requirements.

## Delivery Time and Fees
Typical delivery time is 20 to 35 minutes.

- Standard delivery fee: 8 EUR per order
- No delivery fee for suites and premium packages
- Group orders for two or more rooms may require 40 to 50 minutes

During peak breakfast (07:30-09:30), delivery times can be longer.

## Dietary and Allergy Handling
The kitchen supports vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free requests.

If the guest has an allergy, the order must be marked with the allergen and reconfirmed before preparation. Common allergens handled: nuts, shellfish, egg, soy, milk, and wheat.

## Changes, Cancellations, and Missing Items
Guests may modify or cancel an order within 5 minutes of confirmation.

After food preparation starts, cancellation may include a charge up to 100% of item value.

If an item is missing or incorrect, Room Service will prioritize replacement and waive redelivery fees.

## Contact and Escalation
If delivery exceeds 45 minutes, the order is escalated to the Duty Manager and the guest receives an updated ETA automatically.
