# Maintenance Service Levels and Response Times

## Supported In-Room Issues
Engineering and maintenance support covers:

- Air conditioning and heating
- Television and streaming box
- Wi-Fi and network access points
- In-room safe and keycard power slot
- Lighting, switches, and power outlets
- Bathroom ventilation and basic plumbing

## Priority Levels
### Priority 1 - Critical
Examples: water leak, electrical hazard, door lock failure, no heating in winter.

Target response: within 10 minutes
Target resolution: within 60 minutes or immediate room move decision

### Priority 2 - Urgent
Examples: AC not cooling, no hot water, TV not functioning fully.

Target response: within 20 minutes
Target resolution: within 2 hours

### Priority 3 - Standard
Examples: flickering light, low remote battery, minor fixture noise.

Target response: within 45 minutes
Target resolution: within 6 hours

## Ticket Intake Requirements
Every ticket should capture:

1. Room number
2. Issue category
3. Short issue description
4. Preferred access time
5. Guest contact preference

If the guest is currently in the room, note whether immediate entry is permitted.

## Escalation Rules
A ticket is escalated to Duty Manager when:

- No technician is assigned within target response window
- First repair attempt fails for Priority 1 or Priority 2
- Guest reports repeated issue within 24 hours

Escalated tickets require proactive guest updates every 30 minutes.

## Access and Privacy
Technicians may enter occupied rooms only after guest consent is recorded, except in safety emergencies. For DND rooms, Front Desk coordinates a new access window.

## Completion and Confirmation
When marked resolved, the guest receives a confirmation message with ticket ID and summary of work performed. If unresolved, the assistant should offer room change or further escalation.
