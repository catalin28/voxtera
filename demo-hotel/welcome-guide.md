# Welcome to the Grand Hôtel Lumière

Bienvenue ! We're delighted to host you in the heart of Paris's 8th
arrondissement, a few minutes' walk from the Champs-Élysées and the Place de
la Concorde.

## Your Room

Each room features:

- King or queen Hypnos mattress with Egyptian-cotton linens (430 thread
  count) and a choice of pillow firmness — call housekeeping for a pillow
  menu
- Marble bathroom with a rainfall shower and separate deep-soak tub (in
  Deluxe rooms and above)
- **Diptyque Paris** bath amenities — hand wash, shampoo, conditioner, body
  wash, and body lotion
- Nespresso machine with a complimentary daily refill of pods
- Electric kettle, English Breakfast and Earl Grey tea, biscuits, and
  bottled still and sparkling water (replenished daily)
- Mini-bar — items are scanned automatically; please ask reception if you'd
  prefer it locked
- Smart TV with Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, and live international
  channels
- Universal power sockets and USB-C / USB-A charging at every bedside
- An iron and full-size ironing board in the wardrobe
- A digital safe (instructions in `troubleshooting.md`)

Daily housekeeping is included. Please use the **Do Not Disturb** door
hanger or the button by your door if you'd prefer privacy. A turndown
service is offered in the evenings on request.

## Hotel Facilities

- **Spa Lumière** (lower-ground floor) — pool, hammam, sauna, treatments
- **Le Mirador** (rooftop) — breakfast, lunch, and dinner with views over
  the rooftops of Paris
- **La Petite Terrasse** — all-day dining in the garden courtyard
- **Bar Lumière** (lobby) — cocktails, light bites, and live jazz on
  Thursdays from 21:00
- **Library Lounge** (1st floor) — quiet workspace with a Nespresso bar,
  open 24 hours
- **Boutique** (lobby) — silk scarves, candles, and small Parisian gifts
- **Concierge desk** — staffed 24 hours; we hold a **Clefs d'Or** key
- Complimentary morning shoeshine — leave your shoes in the bag provided
  outside your door before midnight

## Local Recommendations

### Museums (within 20 minutes' walk)

- **Musée d'Orsay** — Impressionist masterpieces, closed Mondays
- **Petit Palais** — free entry to the permanent collection
- **Musée de l'Orangerie** — Monet's Water Lilies, very small queues
  before 11:00
- **Musée Jacquemart-André** — a hidden gem in a 19th-century mansion

### Restaurants we love (5–15 minutes' walk; reservations advised)

- **L'Ardoise** (rue du Mont-Thabor) — classic French bistro, set menu €52
- **Le Cinq** (Four Seasons George V) — three-Michelin-star, special
  occasions
- **Bouillon Pigalle** — affordable French classics, no reservations,
  expect a queue
- **Frenchie** (rue du Nil) — modern French, book weeks ahead
- **Pierre Hermé** (rue Cambon) — the macarons. Trust us.

### Transport

- **Metro**: Concorde (lines 1, 8, 12) and Madeleine (lines 8, 12, 14) are
  both 5 minutes away
- **CDG airport**: 35–55 minutes by taxi (€55–75 fixed fare) or 45 minutes
  via the RER B from Auber
- **Orly airport**: 25–40 minutes by taxi (€32–45 fixed fare)
- **Vélib'** bikes — a station is directly outside; ask the concierge for a
  day pass
- **Uber and Bolt** operate widely; the doorman is happy to call one for
  you

### Day trips

- **Versailles**: 45 minutes by RER C from Invalides; arrive before 10:00
  to beat the queues
- **Giverny** (Monet's gardens): 1h15 by train + shuttle, April to October
  only
- **Reims** (Champagne country): 45 minutes by TGV; visit Taittinger or
  Pommery cellars

The concierge can arrange tickets, private tours, restaurant reservations,
chauffeur-driven cars, and almost anything else you can think of. Just ask.

We hope you have a wonderful stay.
