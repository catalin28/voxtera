# Troubleshooting — In-Room Issues

If something is not working in your room, please dial **extension 0** for the
front desk or **extension 7** for engineering. Most issues can be resolved
within 15 minutes. Common fixes are listed below.

## Television

- The TV is a Samsung smart TV. Press the **red button** on the remote to
  power on; the hotel welcome channel loads first.
- **No picture**: confirm the TV input is set to **HDMI 1**. Press
  `Source` on the remote, then select HDMI 1.
- **No sound**: check the volume bar (lower-right corner) and the mute
  symbol. The remote's mute button is the small crossed-out speaker on the
  upper-left.
- **Frozen image**: hold the power button for 5 seconds to restart.
- **Streaming services** (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video): use the
  `Apps` button. You can sign in with your own account; sessions are wiped
  automatically at check-out.
- The TV remote also controls the **room curtains** — look for the curtain
  icon at the bottom of the remote.

## Air Conditioning & Heating

The room thermostat is on the wall by the bedroom door.

- Tap the screen to wake it.
- Use the **+** and **−** symbols to set the desired temperature
  (range 18–26 °C).
- The fan icon switches between Auto, Low, Medium, and High.
- The system **cannot heat and cool simultaneously** with the windows open.
  If a window or balcony door is open for more than 60 seconds the system
  will pause and a small window icon will appear on the thermostat.
- For deeper adjustments (changing between heating and cooling modes), call
  engineering at **extension 7**.

## Wi-Fi

- Network: **Lumiere-Guest**
- Password: printed on your keycard sleeve at check-in
- If you cannot connect, try **forgetting the network** on your device and
  reconnecting. The hotel router rotates the security key nightly.
- For a higher-bandwidth network suitable for video conferencing or 4K
  streaming, ask reception about **Lumiere-Plus** (free for Lumière Club
  members, €10/day for others).

## Lost or Demagnetised Keycard

- Visit the front desk with your photo ID; a new card is issued
  immediately, free of charge.
- Do **not** store your keycard next to your phone — modern phone cases can
  demagnetise the strip.
- After hours, the night manager can re-encode your card at the lobby
  reception.

## Room Phone

- To call another room: press **R** then the room number.
- To reach the front desk: press **0**.
- To make an external call: press **9**, then the number including country
  code.
- Local calls within Paris are **free**. International calls are charged at
  €1.20 per minute and added to your room bill.

## In-Room Safe

The safe is in the wardrobe.

- To **set a code**: close the door, enter a 4-digit code of your choice,
  and press the `#` key. The green light confirms the code is set.
- To **open**: enter your code and press `#`.
- If you forget your code or the safe will not open, dial extension 0. A
  staff member will arrive within 10 minutes with the master key. You will
  be asked for ID.

## Lights

- The master switch by the door controls all room lights.
- Bedside lamps each have an inline switch.
- The bathroom features motion sensing — wave your hand by the mirror to
  toggle the night-mode dimmer.

## Anything else

If your issue is not listed, dial **extension 0** at any time. Front desk is
staffed 24 hours a day.
