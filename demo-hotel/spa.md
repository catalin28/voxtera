# Spa Lumière — Wellness & Treatments

**Spa Lumière** occupies the lower-ground floor of the hotel and offers a
full menu of treatments, a heated indoor pool, sauna, hammam, and a small
fitness studio.

## Hours

- Treatments: **9:00 – 21:00** daily (last booking at 19:30)
- Pool, sauna & hammam: **6:30 – 22:00**
- Fitness studio: **24 hours** (keycard access)

Reservations are essential for treatments. Please arrive **15 minutes
early** for a robe, slippers, and a brief consultation.

## Pool & Thermal Area

- Indoor heated pool, **18 m**, kept at 29 °C
- Finnish sauna and steam hammam
- Cold plunge and ice fountain
- Relaxation lounge with herbal teas

Children under 16 are welcome in the pool **between 8:00 and 11:00 only**.
The thermal area (sauna, hammam) is **adults only**.

## Fitness Studio

Cardio (Technogym treadmills, bikes, rowers), free weights up to 30 kg, a
functional training rig, and yoga mats. Personal training sessions can be
booked for **€90 per hour** through the spa reception.
